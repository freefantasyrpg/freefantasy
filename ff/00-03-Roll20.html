Roll 20 or higher

Overview

In this system, all checks use a fixed Difficulty Class of 20. Saving throws, attacks, spellcasting checks, skill attempts, and thief abilities must meet or exceed DC 20 to succeed. This creates a unified, easy-to-remember resolution mechanic.

Whenever a character attempts something risky, uncertain, or extraordinary— resisting danger with a saving throw, casting a spell under pressure, picking a lock, disarming a trap, sneaking past a guard, or performing any specialized skill—they must roll against DC 20.

Attacks also use DC 20. Roll a d20, add all modifiers and the target’s Armor Class (AC). If the total is 20 or higher, the attack hits.

Higher Success Thresholds

  • Result 25: Above-average success. Cleaner execution, faster completion, reduced risk, or a small additional benefit depending on the action.
  • Result 30+: Extraordinary success. Masterful execution, significant advantage, or a powerful narrative impact determined by the GM.

This approach simplifies gameplay by removing variable difficulty ratings. Players always know what they need to achieve, and the GM can focus on narrative consequences rather than adjusting numbers. A DC 20 represents a meaningful challenge: success requires competence, luck, or both, making every roll feel significant. The system is easy to teach, fast to use, and consistent across all character types and situations.
